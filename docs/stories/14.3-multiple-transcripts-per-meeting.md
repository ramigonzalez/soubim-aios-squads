# Story 14.3 — Multiple Transcripts per Meeting

**Story ID:** 14.3
**Epic:** E14 — Speaker Review & GPU Transcription
**Priority:** Low | **Effort:** 5 SP (estimate)
**Status:** Draft
**Type:** Feature
**Requested by:** Rami — the comparison viewer used to judge Fathom vs re-transcription

---

## Description

A meeting stores one transcript today (`sources.raw_content`). Keep **several transcripts** per meeting (Fathom original, re-transcribed with speaker review, manual upload) and compare them side by side in the meeting viewer, synced to the recording — the in-app version of the local comparison viewer (`data/tools/build_viewer.py`, out of scope in Story 7.13).

## Acceptance Criteria (draft)

- [ ] `transcripts` (or `source_transcripts`): source_id, kind (`fathom` / `retranscribed` / `uploaded`), content, created_at, is_primary
- [ ] Extraction uses the primary transcript
- [ ] Meeting viewer: choose one transcript or two side by side, both following playback; per-transcript "speaker at this moment" indicator
- [ ] Turn counts and ⚠️ counts per transcript

## Dev Notes

- Pilot comparison (D/SEASON): both transcripts named the same person only 63% of the time; of what Fathom called "Debora", 62% was Debora.
