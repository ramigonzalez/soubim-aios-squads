# Story 12.15 — Invite an External Person to a Project

**Story ID:** 12.15
**Epic:** E12 — Organizations & Client Access
**Priority:** High | **Effort:** 5 SP (estimate)
**Status:** Approved
**Type:** Feature (authorization)
**Requested by:** Rami (2026-10-15), replacing "search organizations" in the project sharing section: "I want Owner A to invite someone outside of his organization."

---

## Story

As an **owner or admin of Organization A**, I want to invite any person by e-mail to one of A's projects, so that clients, consultants and partners can follow it without seeing the rest of A.

## Decisions (Rami, 2026-10-15)

1. The invite is by **e-mail**, to anyone, with or without an account.
2. The project **stays Organization A's**. The invited person gets **external access** to that one project. Their own organization, if they have one, gets nothing.
3. An external person sees **only the projects they were invited to**, never the rest of A.
4. The default access is **Viewer**. A's owners/admins can change it to **Contributor** later, still with limited access (below).
5. **Everyone sees that the person is external** (an "Externo" badge wherever the person appears).
6. Until e-mail is sent (12.14 Resend), the owner/admin **copies the invite link** and sends it themselves.

## Acceptance Criteria

### Invite
- [ ] Project settings get a new section, **"Pessoas externas"**, for owners/admins of the owning organization (pt-BR + en). It has:
  - an e-mail input;
  - an "Convidar" button;
  - after inviting, the **link to copy**, until 12.14 sends it;
  - the list of external people and pending invites: name/e-mail, access (Viewer/Contributor), change access, remove, revoke a pending invite.
- [ ] Inviting an e-mail that is already a regular member of A answers 409 `already_member`. Inviting someone who is already external on the project answers 409 `already_invited`.
- [ ] Invitations reuse 12.5: hashed token, expiry, revoke, one pending invite per e-mail and project.

### Accept
- [ ] **Logged in, or with an account:** the link shows "Organization A invites you to project X", with Accept and Reject. The person must be logged in as the invited e-mail (12.5 rule).
- [ ] **No account:** the link opens sign-up (name and password, or Google 12.8). Then the same Accept/Reject. Sign-up does **not** force creating an organization (see Assumptions).
- [ ] Accepting:
  - makes the person an `external` member of Organization A;
  - assigns them to the project with access `viewer`.
- [ ] Rejecting closes the invite. Organization A sees it as "recusado".

### What an external person can do
- [ ] They see **only** projects they are assigned to; never A's other projects, member list, settings, Drive, Fathom or sharing.
- [ ] **Viewer:** reads what a shared organization sees (12.3/12.4): `shared` meetings only, never `internal`; items per the 12.6 rule for shared organizations.
- [ ] **Contributor:** Viewer, plus adding content the way a shared contributor organization can (12.3). They cannot review, manage, share or invite.
- [ ] The organization switcher (12.5) shows A for them with the "Externo" badge. Their own organization, if any, works as before.

### Everyone sees it
- [ ] An **"Externo" badge** appears:
  - in A's team page;
  - in the project's people list;
  - next to their name on items and meetings they add.

### Removal
- [ ] Removing them from their last project removes the `external` membership. Content they added stays.

### Tests
- [ ] Isolation:
  - an external person on project X gets 403/404 on A's project Y, on A's settings and on internal meetings of X;
  - their list endpoints never include Y or internal content.
- [ ] Viewer cannot write; Contributor can add content but cannot review, manage or share.
- [ ] Accept with an existing account, accept after sign-up, reject, expired, revoked, wrong e-mail logged in.
- [ ] Frontend: the "Pessoas externas" section and the accept page.

## Assumptions & gaps (dev, 2026-10-15)

- **Data model:** `organization_members.role = 'external'` (gives the badge and the switcher entry) plus a `project_members` row for each project. `project_members` needs a new `access` column (`viewer`/`contributor`). `external` joins `ASSIGNED_ROLES`, so `accessible_projects_filter` already limits it to assigned projects. The work is in the **content filters** (12.4 visibility, 12.6 review): they must treat `external` like a shared organization, not like A's own member. That is the security-critical part; it gets an Opus review.
- **Sign-up without an organization:** you said the person "must create an Organization if they don't belong to one, or be part of the Organization". Accepting already makes them part of A (as external), so creating their own organization is **optional** and can be done later. Confirmed by Rami (2026-10-15).
- **Existing org-to-org sharing** ("Organizações com acesso", typing the slug) stays as it is. Confirmed by Rami (2026-10-15).
- **E-mail:** copy the link until 12.14.

## Out of scope (phase 2+)

- Sending the invite by e-mail (12.14 Resend).
- Searching organizations by name (dropped: it would expose the client list).
- An external person inviting others.
- Converting an external person into a regular member (remove, then invite through the team page).
